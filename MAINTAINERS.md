## What maintainers are responsible for

- Triage reports and keep supported behavior and known limitations accurate.
- Review focused changes and run verification appropriate to the risk.
- Protect existing files, credentials, personal data and user-approved spending limits.
- Preserve compatibility or document explicit migrations and backups.
- Review dependency and asset provenance before redistribution.
- Maintain a private security-reporting route and follow up on relevant alerts.
- Record release commits, verification, known issues and rollback instructions.
- Keep access limited to the people and services that need it.
- Treat contributors respectfully and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Communicate availability and arrange handover before an extended absence.

Maintenance is currently best effort. This roster does not establish employment, around-the-clock support, a service-level agreement or a commitment to implement every request.

## Adding an area maintainer

1. Agree on a bounded area and the expected review/triage work.
2. Review the candidate's contributions, judgment, availability and willingness to follow project policies.
3. Obtain the candidate's consent to be listed and to use the published contact route.
4. Record the appointment and scope in a reviewed roster change.
5. Grant the minimum required access separately; verify 2FA and recovery practices for privileged accounts.
6. Review the arrangement after an initial month and adjust scope or access if needed.

Documentation and community maintainers do not automatically need deployment or provider credentials. AI tools may assist maintainers but are not accountable maintainers or independent approving reviewers.

## Planned absence and handover

Before a planned extended absence, record:

- Who has accepted temporary responsibility, for which systems and for how long.
- Open security, data-loss, compatibility and release concerns.
- Pending PRs, release status, backups and rollback procedure.
- Locations and owners of operational runbooks and recovery material.
- How urgent reports reach the acting maintainer.

Transfer credentials only through an approved private mechanism, never a Git issue, Markdown roster or project file. Prefer granting individual access over sharing an account. Revoke temporary access when the handover ends.

The public handover record should contain roles and contact routes, not secrets or recovery codes.

## Unexpected inactivity

If work is stalled, contact [build@flux-ai.co](mailto:build@flux-ai.co) with a concise availability request. For ordinary maintenance, allow at least 14 calendar days before treating non-response as an inactivity concern; this is not a promised response time and does not delay handling an urgent incident.

Where a backup has already been explicitly appointed, that person follows the recorded scope. Silence does not appoint a new lead, grant access or transfer ownership.

Without a designated backup, remaining contributors can document the maintenance status and discuss lawful continuity under the adopted license. The adopted component licenses are documented in [LICENSING.md](docs/LICENSING.md); follow the relevant obligations and preserve third-party rights.

## Removal and access review

A maintainer can step down at any time. The lead can change a role for inactivity, scope changes, misconduct or unsafe handling of privileged access. Record the operational change, preserve relevant private evidence, and provide a private opportunity to respond where appropriate.

Revoke unnecessary repository, hosting, provider and automation permissions. Review credentials the departing person could access and rotate when warranted. Preserve user data, releases and audit records; access removal is not permission to delete them.

Review privileged access at each major release and after an incident or personnel change.

## Conflicts, reporting and continuity gaps

Use [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for conduct concerns and [SECURITY.md](.github/SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

The project mailbox is not an independent ombudsperson channel. A complaint involving the lead requires a non-conflicted handler; none is currently appointed. That limitation must be disclosed, and the lead must not decide a complaint about themselves.

Before a broadly promoted public launch, appoint a consenting recovery contact and establish an independent conduct escalation route. Record real names/handles and contact details only after agreement.
